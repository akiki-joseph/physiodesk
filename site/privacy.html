Junctura

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Junctura is an app for physiotherapists to run their practice: appointments, clients, treatment notes, home exercises and income. It is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, where, who can see it, and how to delete it.

In short: your practice data is yours. It is kept on your phone and in your private account, nobody else's account can read it, we show no ads, we use no tracking, and we never sell or share your data. You can delete everything at any time.

Who is responsible for what

You, the physiotherapist, decide what you record about your clients and why. For that information you are responsible to your clients (for example, for having their consent), and Junctura stores and processes it only on your behalf, to provide the app to you. Client records can include health information, so please follow the professional and data protection rules of your country, such as Lebanon's Law No. 81 of 2018.

What Junctura stores

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our database provider; nobody can read it, including us. We also store your plan and trial dates.
  • Your practice records: what you enter in the app, such as your clients' names, phone numbers, addresses, birthdays, assessments, diagnoses, treatment notes, measurements, consent signatures, home exercise programs, appointments, prices, payments and expenses, and your settings for documents (name, title, registration number, phone).
  • Client exercise pages: when you send a client their home exercises, a copy of that program is stored under a long random code: the client's first name, your name, title and phone number, the exercises, how often, and your advice. If the client ticks exercises as done, the days are stored too (for 90 days).
  • Only on your phone, never uploaded: your PIN, theme, calendar link, when and how reminders are sent, and the backup files you choose to save.

Junctura does not collect your location, your contacts list, your photos, or anything about how you use the app. It has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Phone permissions

  • Calendar (if you turn it on): to put your sessions in your phone's calendar and to import sessions you already have there. Other calendar events stay on your phone.
  • Notifications: to remind you of sessions and of client reminders to send.
  • SMS (only in the version installed directly from our website, not in the Google Play version, and only if you turn it on): to send the appointment reminders you set up to your clients, from your own number, at the time you chose.

Where it is kept

Your data is kept on your phone and, when you have an account, in our cloud database hosted by Supabase, so it is safe if you lose your phone and can be used on another phone. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored encrypted by the provider. Database rules make each account's records readable and changeable only by that account. Account emails (such as confirming your address or a sign-in code) are sent through our email provider. Our website, including client exercise pages, is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives the usual technical information of any web visit, such as an IP address.

Who can see it

  • You, on any phone where you sign in to your account.
  • Your client, only their own exercise page, through the link you send them. Anyone who has that link can open that page, so send it only to the client.
  • Us, only if needed to keep the service running, to help you when you ask, or when the law requires it. We do not look through your records otherwise.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

When you send something from Junctura, such as a reminder, an invoice or an exercise link, it goes through the app you choose (WhatsApp, SMS, email…) and that app's own privacy terms apply.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete my account), or by asking us; see Delete your account. This deletes your account, your records and your client exercise pages straight away. Copies in our providers' backups, if any, disappear when those backups expire, within 30 days. Backup files you saved yourself, and the copy on your phone until you uninstall the app, stay under your control.

Your rights

You can see and correct everything in the app, save a full copy of it (Settings → Backup), and delete it. If you have a question about your data, or want us to delete it, write to us at junctura.app@gmail.com. Clients who want to see, correct or delete what their physiotherapist recorded should ask their physiotherapist first.

Age

Junctura is for professionals and is not meant for anyone under 18. Records about clients who are children are the responsibility of the physiotherapist, who needs a parent's or guardian's consent.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top, and for important changes we tell you in the app before they apply.
