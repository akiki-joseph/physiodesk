Delete your Junctura account

How to delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Junctura and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Junctura account". We confirm by replying, and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, password and plan.
  • All your practice records: clients, notes, appointments, measurements, consents, programs, payments and expenses.
  • Your clients' exercise pages; their links stop working.

Copies in our providers' backups, if any, disappear when those backups expire, within 30 days. Backup files you saved yourself stay with you, and the copy on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
